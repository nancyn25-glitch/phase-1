Maggi

Ingredients

  1. Noodles
  2. oil
  3. Water
  4. Meggi masala
  5. Onion
  6. Tomato

Instruction

  1. put 2 spoon of oil in pen
  2. put onion ,chilli and tomato in hot oil
  3. after frying put 2 cup of water
  4. after boiling water put noddles in that water then leave it for 3-4 mai in a medium flame.
